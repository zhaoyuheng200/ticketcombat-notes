# TicketCombat.com — field notes

**TicketCombat.com** was a free website that taught Ontario drivers how to fight
traffic and parking tickets themselves, without hiring a paralegal. An anonymous
author started it in 2008 and kept it online, supported by ads, for 17 years. It
walked readers through the whole Provincial Offences Act process in five steps,
from deciding to fight to cross-examining the officer at trial. People regularly
recommended it on Ontario car and deal forums.

The site **went offline in late 2025**. The domain is still registered, but its
hosting account is gone, so every page now shows a hosting-company error. The
Wayback Machine still holds nearly all of it.

This repository is an **unofficial guide to that site**: what it covered, how it
was organized, what has changed since it was written, and a direct link to the
archived copy of every page.

> - **Not affiliated** with TicketCombat.com or its author.
> - **Not a mirror, and no download.** The site's terms forbid republishing its
>   content, so these pages summarize in our own words and link to the archived

> - **Not legal advice, and often out of date.** Most of the site was written in
>   2008–2011, and Ontario's process has changed since. See
>   [What's changed](whats-changed.md) before relying on anything here or there.
>   If you have a real ticket, check the court office or municipality on your
>   ticket, or talk to a licensed paralegal or lawyer.
